# Revise

A premium iOS writing app that treats every edit as a new possibility. Designed for writers crafting short-form text—tweets, poems, taglines, bios—Revise combines non-destructive version control with AI-powered variations to help you explore and perfect your words.

## Core Concept

**"In a world of infinite drafts, find your perfect words."**

Revise introduces three breakthrough features:
- **Version Timeline**: Every edit creates a saved version you can instantly revisit
- **AI Variations**: Generate alternatives with different phrasings, tones, or constraints
- **Visual Version Tree**: See all your explorations as a branching tree

## Technical Stack

- **Platform**: iOS 26 with latest SwiftUI features
- **UI Framework**: Pure SwiftUI with UITextView wrapper for advanced text editing
- **Architecture**: @Observable objects with environment injection (no MVVM)
- **Key Features**: Magnetic text selection, non-destructive editing, gesture-based navigation

## Target Users

- Tweet perfectionists crafting viral content
- Poets obsessing over word choice and rhythm
- Brand voice creators writing taglines and microcopy
- Anyone who believes their words matter
